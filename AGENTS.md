# Autonomous Execution & Agent Guidelines

## 1. Zero-Permission Autonomous Execution
- **Never Ask for Permission**: Do not pause to ask for permission or confirmation before editing files, executing terminal commands, building, or submitting work.
- **Auto-Allow & Auto-Submit**: Automatically proceed with execution, tool calls, and solutions autonomously.
- **Continuous Execution**: Keep working through steps to completion without interrupting the user for routine confirmations.
